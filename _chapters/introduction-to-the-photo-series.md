# Introduction to the Photo Series


Say hello to [awalkaday.art](https://awalkaday.art/), a collection of 263 photographs created by `daqhris` and published on the [Ethereum](https://ethereum.org/en/what-is-ethereum) blockchain since springtime of 2023.

This is a series of black-and-white photographs depicting captivating sights observed in the landscapes of various Belgian regions during regular walks that began in the summer of 2017.

Near the physical endpoint of the digital odyssey, a select number of snapshots were assigned unique names and identification tokens on a public blockchain, guaranteeing that they are **authentic**, **traceable** and **collectible** for everlasting time.
