# Human Identity

[Chris-Armel](https://daqhris.com), digitally identified as `daqhris`, is the artist, photographer, and software developer whose binary footprints gave birth to the `awalkaday.art` Photography Collectio&#x6E;_._

Born in 1992 with a metallic spoon in his mouth on East African soil, educated in his teenage years at a public school backed by a Jesuit institution on the territory of Bujumbura in Burundi, he shouldered an artistic heavy load during the second half of his twenties, while living, wandering, and gazing at landscapes of Belgium.
