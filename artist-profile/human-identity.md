# Human Identity

[Chris-Armel](https://daqhris.com), digitally identified as `daqhris`, is the artist, photographer and software developer whose binary footprints gave birth to the `awalkaday.art` Photography Collectio&#x6E;_._

Born in 1992 with a metallic spoon in his mouth on East African soil, and educated in his teenage years at a public school and a Jesuit institution on the territory of Bujumbura in Burundi, he pursued an artistic passion in the midst of his twenties, when living, wandering and gazing at landscapes of Belgium.
